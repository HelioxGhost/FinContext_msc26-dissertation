Conversely, early‑year quarters typically see lower order volumes due to reduced student‑driven demand. The firm's reporting of a 33 % QoQ revenue growth in the most recent quarter (revenueGrowthQuarterlyYoy 33.11 %) underscores this pattern.

* revenueGrowthQuarterlyYoy 33.11 % (basic financials).

Seasonality drives operating income swings; academic calendar effects are especially pronounced in college towns where a significant share of orders comes from students.
