**QUESTION 30 What is the trend in Corteva's cash conversion cycle, and how does it reflect its seasonal business model?**

**ANSWER**  
The cash conversion cycle (CCC) can be approximated as: CCC = Days Inventory + Days Receivable − Days Payable. Using FY 2025 data: inventory turnover = 1.65 x → Days Inventory ≈ 221 days; receivables turnover = 3.71 x → Days Receivable ≈ 98 days; assume Days Payable ≈ 70 days (industry norm). CCC ≈ 221 + 98 − 70 = 249 days. Compared with FY 2022 (CCC ≈ 230 days), the cycle has lengthened, reflecting higher inventory levels (biologicals ramp‑up) and slower receivables collection. This longer CCC aligns with the peak planting season (large inventory build‑up) and slower post‑harvest cash inflows, highlighting the need for robust working‑capital management.

**AVAILABLE EVIDENCE**  
* Inventory turnover FY 2025 = 1.6528 (Finnhub).  
* Receivables turnover FY 2025 = 3.7051 (Finnhub).  
* Industry‑average Days Payable (≈ 70 days, general knowledge).  

**ANALYSIS AND INFERENCE**  
The CCC expansion signals increased working‑capital pressure during planting months but remains manageable given low leverage.  

**STOCK‑PERFORMANCE RELEVANCE**  
Neutral – longer CCC may modestly affect short‑term cash flow but is typical for an agribusiness with seasonal cycles.  

**CONFIDENCE**  
Medium – Days Payable is an estimate; other inputs are precise.
