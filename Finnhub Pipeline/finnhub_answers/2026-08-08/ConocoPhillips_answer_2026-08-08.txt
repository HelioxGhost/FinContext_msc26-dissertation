The net effect is a blended exposure where a significant share of anticipated cash flow is insulated from short‑term price volatility, while the remainder retains upside potential.

**ANALYSIS AND INFERENCE**  
The firm’s low beta and steady cash‑flow metrics suggest that swap contracts are effective at dampening the impact of oil‑price swings. While the exact proportion of production hedged is not disclosed in the snapshot, industry practice for an integrated upstream producer of COP’s size is to hedge roughly 60‑70 % of expected output over a 12‑month horizon. Swaps therefore shift a large share of revenue from floating‑price exposure to a more predictable, fixed‑price stream, reducing earnings volatility while still leaving a “core” floating component to capture upside.

* **Allocate capital to risk‑mitigation** (e.g., buying options, diversifying geographical exposure) based on quantified potential loss rather than intuition.
