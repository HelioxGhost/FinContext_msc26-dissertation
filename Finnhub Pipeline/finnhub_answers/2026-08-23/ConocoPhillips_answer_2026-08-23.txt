Monte Carlo simulation is layered on top of the VaR model to generate a distribution of possible price paths for underlying commodities, incorporating volatility, mean‑reversion, and correlation assumptions. This enables the risk‑management team to:

1. **Stress‑Test Extreme Scenarios** – By running thousands of simulated price trajectories, the Monte Carlo engine reveals tail‑risk events (e.g., a 30 % oil‑price drop) that the static VaR metric might under‑represent.  
2. **Assess Portfolio Sensitivity** – The simulation outputs “greeks” (delta, gamma) for the company’s hedging book, showing how existing oil‑price swaps or collars would perform under volatile market moves.  
3. **Inform Capital Allocation** – Knowing the potential loss distribution helps the treasury decide the appropriate size of risk‑capital reserves and the level of hedge needed to keep earnings volatility within target ranges.  
4. **Regulatory Compliance** – VaR results are reported to regulators (e.g., SEC, CFTC) as part of the company’s market‑risk disclosure, demonstrating that the firm maintains risk‑management standards.

**AVAILABLE EVIDENCE**

**ANALYSIS AND INFERENCE**  
*Verified:* None in the snapshot; the explanation relies on typical industry practice for a large integrated oil company.
