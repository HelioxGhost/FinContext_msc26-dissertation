A shift toward wearables and “smart‑device” ecosystems can **increase engagement with Google Services** (e.g., voice‑search, streaming, app installs) while potentially reducing traditional mobile‑ad impressions. However, the recent decision to **phase out Pixel production in China by 2027** (see news on 2026‑08‑18) may modestly dent smartphone volumes in that market, offset by growth in emerging markets and the expanding wearables segment. Overall, as long as the Android ecosystem retains a critical mass of active devices, Google Services revenue growth should stay **near‑term robust**, with a **mid‑term upside** driven by wearables‑centric services and the integration of AI features across device categories.
