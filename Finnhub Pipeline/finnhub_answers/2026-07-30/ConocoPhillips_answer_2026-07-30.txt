Together, these tools let the company:

* Identify the size of potential losses under stressed market scenarios.  
* Stress‑test the balance sheet (e.g., impact on cash flow, debt‑service coverage).

The outputs are reported to senior leadership and the board, shaping capital‑allocation, dividend policy and risk‑capital buffers.
