**AVAILABLE EVIDENCE**  
- No DMA‑specific entry in the Finnhub snapshot, but the question references ongoing regulatory scrutiny.  
- Recent news items (e.g., “Google Joins Apple…” and “Alphabet, Microsoft, Meta Platforms, Apple…” from July 2026) show heightened focus on Big‑Tech platform rules.  
- Stable knowledge: The DMA entered into force in November 2023 and applies to firms meeting the gatekeeper thresholds, a category that includes Apple.

**ANALYSIS AND INFERENCE**  
*Verified evidence*: The DMA’s statutory requirements (commission caps, interoperability, data‑access rules).  
*Inference*: Apple’s existing App Store model—mandatory use of Apple‑managed payments with a 15‑30 % cut—conflicts with the DMA’s “fair, open, and interoperable” mandate. The regulator could force Apple to (i) lower fees, (ii) open the App Store to third‑party payment processors, and (iii) accept “alternative distribution” for certain apps, thereby reducing a key revenue stream.
