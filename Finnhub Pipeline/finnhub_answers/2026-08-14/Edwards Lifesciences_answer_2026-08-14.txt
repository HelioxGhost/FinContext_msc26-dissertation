**DIRECT ANSWER**  
The Finnhub snapshot does not provide a **fair‑value estimate** for Edwards Lifesciences’ **in‑process research and development (IPR&D) assets**, nor does it list the specific projects included. Generally, IPR&D fair value is determined by management using a discounted‑cash‑flow (DCF) analysis of the expected future cash flows from the acquired technology, adjusted for the probability of regulatory approval and market adoption. Key milestones that typically must be achieved before the IPR&D can be **amortized** include:  

1. **Regulatory Clearance** – FDA (or comparable) approval for the underlying product or technology.  
2. **Commercial Launch** – commencement of sales in at least one major market (U.S. or EU).  
3. **Revenue Ramp‑Up** – achievement of a defined revenue threshold (e.g., $10‑20 M in annual sales) which confirms commercial viability.  

Only after these milestones are satisfied does the accounting standard (ASC 350‑40) require the IPR&D to be transferred from the balance‑sheet “intangible‑asset” category to a **finite‑life amortizable asset**, where it will be expensed over its estimated useful life (often 5‑10 years). Until then, the IPR&D remains non‑amortizable and is tested for impairment each quarter.
