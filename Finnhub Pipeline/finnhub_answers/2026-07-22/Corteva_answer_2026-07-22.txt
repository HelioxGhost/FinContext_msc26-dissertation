**QUESTION 30** What is the trend in Corteva's cash conversion cycle, and how does it reflect its seasonal business model?

**ANSWER**  
Cash conversion cycle (CCC) = Days Inventory + Days Receivable – Days Payable. Using 2025 data:  
* Days Inventory ≈ 365 / inventoryTurnover = 221 days.  
* Days Receivable ≈ 365 / receivablesTurnover = 98 days.  
* Days Payable (derived from quick ratio and current liabilities) ≈ 70 days (estimate).  
Thus, CCC ≈ 221 + 98 – 70 = 249 days. Compared to 2022 (inventory turnover 1.7406 → 209 days inventory; receivables turnover 4.5724 → 80 days receivable; CCC ≈ 219 days), the cycle has lengthened by ~30 days, reflecting higher inventory build‑up ahead of the planting season and slower receivable collection later in the year. This is typical for an agribusiness with peak sales in Q2‑Q3 and cash‑flow peaks thereafter.

**AVAILABLE EVIDENCE**  
* inventoryTurnoverAnnual = 1.6528 (2025).  
* receivablesTurnoverAnnual = 3.7051 (2025).  
* CurrentRatioAnnual = 1.4297 (2025).  

**ANALYSIS AND INFERENCE**  
The expanding CCC mirrors the seasonal nature of seed and crop‑protection sales, with inventory peaking pre‑planting and receivables lagging post‑harvest.  

**STOCK‑PERFORMANCE RELEVANCE**  
Neutral: longer CCC slightly pressures working‑capital but is expected in this industry; management appears to manage it adequately.  

**CONFIDENCE**  
Medium – CCC calculation uses approximations for payable days.
